Safety First Culture

Zero-Harm High Voltage Safety Architecture

High voltage electrical work demands uncompromising safety protocols. At Mathurai Electricals & Contractors, our engineers adhere to the strictest international safety codes to ensure 100% accident-free sites.

Permit To Work (PTW)

Strict line isolation, double earth discharging, and signed permit-to-work protocols prior to any high-voltage maintenance.

40 Cal/cm² Arc Flash PPE

Certified electrical PPE suites, insulated flash suits, 33kV insulating rubber mats, and calibrated voltage detector probes.

Deep Chemical Earthing Grids

Maintenance of earth resistance values < 1 Ohm via treated copper-bonded chemical earth electrodes and meshed earth grounding mats.

Daily Toolbox Safety Briefings

Mandatory morning safety briefings and Hazard Identification & Risk Assessment (HIRA) for every on-site crew member.
